What the plant's people see.

The configuration console decides what a plant watches and what work it runs. These are the screens that set-up turns into, on the phone of the Operator who does the work and of the Lead who is told when it slips. Nothing on them is typed in for the screen: each one is drawn from the plant's set-up.

How to review

Six stops, in the order a day runs. Each opens the screens for that stop, and says which console setting drives them. The phone at the top is live: tap through it, or jump it to any stop.

  1. Today at the plantDriven by the structure (unit processes and their equipment) and each one's criticality.
  2. A task and its flowDriven by the task lines the plant accepted and the flow linked to each task in the library.
  3. An alert becomes an issueDriven by the limits and times on a reading, and the severity of the limit crossed.
  4. The fix flowDriven by the root causes of the issue and the fix flow each cause carries.
  5. What the Lead seesDriven by the criticality timelines (late tasks) and the severity timelines (issues not started).
  6. LanguageDriven by the operator text kept per language: English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil.

Kept from that design

  • Search looks inside the plant you are in, and says so in a strip under the field.
  • A result shows the match plus one level below it, and each item once.
  • Typed letters are marked in the rows that matched. Two letters is the least.
  • The bar at the bottom: Overview, Issues, Tasks, Report.
  • Back is an arrow at the top left, 44 px.

Added, because the console now sets it

  • Criticality beside every unit process, piece of equipment, task and issue. Severity stays a separate label.
  • Sensors on a pipe are listed with the unit process they take part in.
  • A task's kind: routine check, routine work, manual testing, preventive maintenance, calibration, condition-based.
  • The step-by-step screen, shared by a task's flow and an issue's fix flow.
  • The Lead's view of what is late, with the times that got it there.

Not rebuilt here

  • The list of all plants and its filter chips, for people with several plants.
  • Voice search, the sheet that changes where search looks, loading and no-signal states.
  • Diagnostic flows: this build has the person pick the cause, then follow its fix flow.
  • Sign-in and who may see what: that comes with the roles work.

Their wording follows the same one change: a unit process is never called a stage.
