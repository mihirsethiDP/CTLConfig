v7/1

The recommended approach

Takes a plant from the library to live running, end to end, in plain words. Where Alex's model, v6 or Mihir's earlier direction disagree, it takes the better option and says why on a card.

  • Each tank holds its own readings, issues and tasks.
  • One checklist feeds Readiness, go live and Coverage.
  • New alerts run a quiet trial; a real Emergency during commissioning still reaches the Leads, with no call and no hooter.
  • Every value a plant changes is kept with its reason, and a library change shows where it lands before it is published.

v7/2

Alex's model as drafted

In build

Alex's configuration model, built as Alex drafted it, costs included, so the two can be compared screen by screen. Only what Alex's model changes is changed, and it is never quietly improved with v7/1's recommendations. Where the drafts are silent or say two things, v7/2 takes the reading Alex's own ranked principles favour, and the challenges doc lists each one.

  • Equipment grouped into treatment steps (Alex's unit processes, stages and control groups).
  • A new plant set up in two review passes: first its structure, then what each part watches.
  • Nothing is sent to anyone until the plant is Live, Emergencies included.
  • Library versions a plant can stay on, and Alex's words on screen.
The prototype and its hand-off will open from here once they are built. Until then, every card in the challenges doc says what v7/2 will build.
v7/3

The decided build

Builds the calls made on the decision cards, 28 of the 29 so far. It starts from v7/1 and adds unit processes and control groups from the plant graph, nothing sent on a new plant, the threshold bar with what each range triggers, and a Flows page to read, try and edit diagnostic flows and the steps of every job. Its hand-off says which calls are built in part.

What is the same in both

  • The four plants: DigitalPaani, read from its real SCADA layout, and three sample plants, Vedanta ETP, Manesar STP and Bawal WTP, each carrying the same part of the story.
  • One story clock, starting Thu 29 Oct 2026, 09:00, and the same twelve moments from the library to a library change.
  • Sample readings, generated the same way every time, and one engine that counts trial firings and opens issues.
  • The database's 56 equipment types under their stored names, and the workbook's 31 issues, 183 root causes with their fixes and 75 general tasks.
  • The same challenge cards, from one shared file, and your calls on them: a call made anywhere shows everywhere in this browser.

How to compare

Start with the challenges doc: each card says what v7/1 builds and what v7/2 builds, with links to the same place in each prototype. Inside a prototype, a quiet "Decisions on this screen" note under the page header names the challenges that arise there and links to them.

Once v7/2 is built, each prototype's story will open the same moment in the other version in its own tab. The two keep separate stories, so resetting one does not touch the other; your calls are shared.
