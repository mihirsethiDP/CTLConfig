3New in v7/3

  • Flows. The top bar has a Flows page with the diagnostic flows, the fix flows and the task flows. Open one to read it, try it by answering as an operator would, or edit it: add, reorder and remove steps, send each answer to another question or to a root cause, and give a root cause its fix flow. The page lists what is left to finish before you save. Open Flows.
  • Structure. Each plant has a Structure tab: its unit processes by stage, the equipment that takes part in each, its control groups, and the equipment not placed yet. DigitalPaani's proposed structure.
  • What each range triggers. On a reading's limits, the bar has four handles and each range has a Triggers slot: an issue with its diagnostic flow, or a task and where it is filed. Open BL-1 and choose Change limits.

5How to run and reset

Open prototype.html. v7/3 keeps its story in its own browser storage (ctl73.estate.v1, ctl73.overlay.v1), so playing it never changes v7/1. "Reset the story" in the top bar starts it again, and puts every flow back as the library shipped it. Your own calls in v7/3 are kept under ctl73.decisions. The calls made live in assets/calls73.js. Checks: node tests/smoke.js and node tests/v73.test.js from this folder.
