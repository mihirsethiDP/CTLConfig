How to answer

  1. Read a card: the question, the recommendation, what v7/1 and v7/2 each build, and the options with what each one leads to. What Alex's pack says, what v6 does today, Mihir's earlier direction and what to tell Alex are folded under Background.
  2. Pick your call under the options: one of them, or "Something else". Pick it again to take it back.
  3. When you are done, use Copy my answers at the top of this page and paste the text into your reply. Cards without a call are listed at the end.

Your calls are kept in this browser only. They are shared with the Decisions pages of both prototypes and with the hand-off docs, so a call made in one place shows in all of them. To add a note to a call, open the card in the prototype with "Add a note on this card".

Each card says when it arose: in the first review of Alex's packs, while reviewing v7/1, or while designing v7/2. New challenges are added here as they come up, and their markers appear on the screens where they arise.
