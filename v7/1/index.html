1What v7/1 is

One clickable prototype that carries a plant from the library, through commissioning, to live running and a library change, with every screen in plain words.

v7/1 keeps v6's content: the database's 56 equipment types under their stored names, the workbook's 31 issues, 183 root causes with a fix for each, 75 general tasks, and the four sample plants. It replaces v6's nine setup steps with a plant that is either Commissioning or Live, a Readiness home fed by one checklist, a quiet trial before go live, a record of every value a plant changes, and a library that shows where a change lands before it is published.

Mihir's brief for v7: simple by design, frictionless, plain words, one home per fact, show the consequence before it happens, and critique rather than follow, whether the source is Alex's pack, v6 or Mihir's own earlier calls. Each place where those sources disagree is a decision card.

5What is simulated

  • Readings. Every reading is a sample series, worked out the same way every time from the sensor and the time, polled every 15 minutes in this build (D5 recommends 1 minute). Each count or preview built from them carries Sample readings. DigitalPaani's structure is real; its readings are samples too.
  • The SCADA read. DigitalPaani is the exported SCADA layout (drawing v157, site labelled Ireo Uptown). "Read again" is not wired. The form answers on five of its equipment (EQTT-1, EQTT-2, FFP-1, FFP-2, UFFP-1) are stand-ins.
  • One user. Everyone is the Global Admin; there is no role picker, so who may do what (Full or Technical Admin, a Lead with a grant) is not shown.
  • The User Center. A read-only stub built from v6's rosters. "Edit in the User Center" only shows a message.
  • Messages. Nothing is sent. An issue records who would have been told and how (WhatsApp, in the app, a call, the hooter).
  • The operator app. Not part of v7. Issue and task pages stand in for the work, and the equipment page's Phone view stands in for the Equipment Card.
  • Time. One story clock for every plant, starting Thu 29 Oct 2026, 09:00 IST. Move time on (+1 hour to +1 week) runs the engine for every plant; Activity is stamped with the same clock.
  • The library. v6's content, every type at version 1 except Clarifier Tank, already at version 2 (published Tue 20 Oct: Sludge blanket high must hold for 5 → 15 min), so SC-1 starts as "Library changed since".
  • Storage. In the browser only: ctl7.estate.v1 and ctl7.overlay.v1 (the story), ctl7.story (story panel open or closed), ctl7.decisions (your calls, shared with this document, the challenges doc and v7/2).

Each criterion is one check in the prototype, starting from Reset the story. They follow the 12 chapters. Where a decision card recommends something v7/1 does not do yet, the criterion checks what v7/1 does now; the card says what would change.

10 · A library change Open

7How to run and reset

  1. Open site/v7/1/prototype.html in a browser, from any static server or straight from disk. There is no build step and no library: plain JavaScript, with Figtree and IBM Plex Mono from Google Fonts.
  2. Start at All Plants. The Story button opens the 12 chapters; each has "Take me there" and links to its decisions.
  3. Reset the story (top bar) puts every plant back to Thu 29 Oct, 09:00 and clears everything changed in the story. It does not clear your calls on the Decisions page; use "Clear my call" on a card, or remove ctl7.decisions from the browser's storage.
  4. Checks, from site/v7/1 with Node: node tests/smoke.js renders every page for every plant and fails on errors, banned words and dead links; node tests/core7.test.js checks the story's outcomes; node tests/decisions7.test.js checks the decision cards, their links and this document; node tests/integrate7.test.js walks the twelve chapters in one session and checks that every module's pages agree. From site/v7, node tests/docs.test.js checks this document, the overview and the challenges doc.
